# PairVoice V1 System Plan
## Revenue objective
Move qualified pairs to client-approved work with low rejection, fast cycle time and protected contribution.

## Proof targets
1. Synthetic pair completes the full workflow without direct DB edits.
2. Two real Spain pairs complete it.
3. Ten approved pairs validate acceptance, support load, cycle time and contribution.
4. Automate the measured bottleneck after evidence.

## Priorities
Clean DB baseline; credential inventory/assignment; working admin; seven-session tracking; internal/client QA; immutable earnings/payout idempotency; referral qualification; outbox automation; revenue alerts.
